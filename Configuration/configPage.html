Monochrome Music Settings

Configure your Monochrome Music streaming and TIDAL HiFi integration for Jellyfin 12.0.

Enables direct high-speed catalog querying and token generation via TIDAL OAuth/Client Credentials.
Specify a personal HiFi or TIDAL token if you wish to override automatic authentication.
Shows "Monochrome Music" in the Jellyfin Channels section for browsing top tracks, albums, and searching.
When enabled, audio data flows through Jellyfin rather than redirecting clients directly to the CDN (useful if clients are behind restrictive firewalls).
